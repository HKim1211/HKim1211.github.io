About

I am a science and technology policy researcher at KISTEP, working on data-driven analysis of Korea's national R&D system. I trained as a condensed matter physicist, earning my Ph.D. from Jeonbuk National University in 2020, and joined KISTEP the same year.

At KISTEP I have served as principal investigator of the Korea R&D Survey (2021–2022) and the Analysis on Government R&D Investment (2023–2025), and I currently lead the Government R&D Performance Analysis. Working with these datasets has shaped my interest in how better data and analytics can support evidence-based science and technology policy.

Having done research in the lab myself, I also try to keep a researcher's point of view on how funding and evaluation systems work in practice.
